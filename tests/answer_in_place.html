01 · A SMALL FAVOR

The mixed-up checks. Four regulars, four suppers, and a gust of wind through the door that sent every ticket flying across the floor of the diner just after closing time, when June was already stacking the chairs and the rain on Route 9 had turned the parking lot into a mirror for the neon sign that still buzzed above the counter.

Nora sits by the window. Luis sits at the counter. Ada and Ben share the corner booth and argue about the jukebox, which has played the same three songs since nineteen seventy-one and will play them again tomorrow night.

June waits for you to ask.

The clock on the wall says ten past eleven.
